## Project Overview — Momarché

**Momarché** was a platform that uses **group purchasing** as a launchpad for **food discovery, distribution, and online food business creation**, helping busy professionals access daily essentials at lower cost while saving time and cognitive effort.

The product addresses a common but underserved problem: for working professionals, food procurement is **time-consuming, repetitive, expensive, and mentally draining**. Limited variety, inefficient purchasing flows, and the daily burden of cooking, cleaning, and planning create constant friction—pulling focus away from work, health, and personal life.
